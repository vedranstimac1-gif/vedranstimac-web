Mural of "Solidarity with The People of Palestine"

For decades the State of Israel has carried out systematic oppression and ethnic cleansing of the Palestinian population: land grabs, forced displacement, cuts to electricity and water supplies, blockades of goods and services (for example, a ban on importing Covid vaccines for medical staff), and the bombing of the Gaza Strip, where in 2021 alone 256 people were killed, among them 66 children and 40 women. In the face of injustice it is important not to stay silent, because silence means standing on the side of the oppressor.

The mural was painted on a residential building in Sisak as a call to end Israeli aggression, as solidarity with the people of Palestine, and as a hope for a brighter, more peaceful and freer future for everyone in Palestine and Israel who suffers violence and daily injustice. The silhouette of the portrait belongs to Leila Khaled, Palestinian activist and intellectual.

Sisak, Croatia (2021)
