ABOUT

After graduating in economics at the Faculty of Economics and Tourism in Pula, I moved to Ljubljana to pursue a PhD in political anthropology, researching peasant social movements. I came close to finishing it, but chose to step away and devote myself fully to art. Those years still shape everything I make.

My whole body of work is rooted in social themes and social problems. Through art – on walls, on paper and on canvas – I try to bring them into public discourse and make room for the stories that history tends to leave out.

I have been working in illustration since 2014, where my main focus has been the portrait: through it I try to convey the ideas and the life of the person portrayed and to build a story behind every image. Alongside illustration I paint murals and make screen prints, and I tattoo as a member of the Jutarnja šetnja tattoo collective in Zagreb. Over the years I have worked for numerous clients, both through illustration and through murals. Since 2025 my focus has shifted to painting: in oil on canvas I explore the everyday lives of ordinary people, most recently in the series Vikendom rekreativno.

I am the co-organiser of Art Park Pula and the organiser of the street art festival Kistom kroz zid in Pula.
